# Technical Support

For technical support, product-related issues, or feedback, please contact the **ThingsLinker support team**.

Our support team will review your request and respond within **1–2 working days**. Please provide a clear description of your issue so we can assist you efficiently.


### Contact Us

For technical assistance, email us at **support@thingslinker.com**.

For sales, product enquiries, or quotations, contact **sales@thingslinker.com**.

**Working Time:** 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
