# What is ESP32?

The ESP32 is a powerful and low-cost System-on-Chip (SoC) developed by Espressif Systems for Internet of Things (IoT) and embedded applications. It combines a microcontroller, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth connectivity, memory, and a wide range of peripherals into a single chip.

Since its introduction, the ESP32 has become one of the most popular microcontroller platforms for makers, students, engineers, and product developers due to its excellent balance of performance, connectivity, and affordability.

ESP32 boards are commonly used in:

- Home Automation
- Industrial Automation
- Robotics
- Smart Agriculture
- Wireless Sensor Networks
- Data Logging Systems
- IoT Products
- Wearable Devices

---

## Key Features of ESP32

- Dual-Core Processor
- Built-in Wi-Fi Connectivity
- Bluetooth Support
- Multiple GPIO Pins
- Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC)
- PWM Output
- UART, SPI, and I2C Communication
- Low Power Consumption
- Arduino, ESP-IDF, and MicroPython Support

---

## Why is ESP32 Popular?

Traditional microcontrollers such as Arduino Uno require external Wi-Fi or Bluetooth modules for wireless communication.

The ESP32 integrates these features directly into the chip, reducing cost, complexity, and power consumption while providing significantly more processing power and memory.
